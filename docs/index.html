Live updates (optional)

GitHub's own timer often skips runs. With an Actions token here, Refresh asks the engine for fresh prices during ASX hours (about a minute). Use a fine-grained token limited to your etf-signal repository with “Actions: read and write” permission.

Trading mode

In paper mode the tool only tracks a $10,000 paper portfolio. In real-money mode it also gives you your own orders, sized from what you have actually recorded buying and selling, and your alerts become those orders.

In real-money mode, set the investment amount below to the cash you've put aside for this strategy. Record every trade with “I did this” so your next orders are right.

Starting amount

Only used until you log money in and out on the Overview (“Add money”). After that, the tool uses what you've actually put in, on the days you put it in.

Between $500 and $5,000,000. Change it any time, for example when you add money.
